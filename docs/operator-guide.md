# Operator guide — MNG

Portal: no single self-service e-procurement portal -- an investor first
registers a legal entity (LLC) with the General Authority for State
Registration ('LERO' in Invest Mongolia's own English-language guide),
decided within 10 business days. Post-registration, the entity must
separately register with the relevant tax and social-insurance offices
(NOT bundled into the entity-registration certificate). Foreign
investors face an additional minimum-capital rule (USD 100,000 per
foreign investor holding 25%+ of issued shares, Art.3.1.5 of the
Investment Law) and, ONLY when the investor is itself 50%+
foreign-state-owned AND the proposed stake is 33%+ of the Mongolian
entity AND the sector is mining, banking and finance, or publication/
media/communication, a prior Ministry of Economy and Development (MED)
permission requirement. This is a narrow gate, not a general FDI
screening regime -- Mongolia's own 2012-era Strategic Entities Foreign
Investment Law (SEFIL) was broader and has since been replaced. Filings
that trigger the foreign-state-owned-entity gate (per the engagement's
own declared ownership/sector figures, independently recomputed) but
lack MED permission verification are held before a filing can proceed.
Entity: the General Authority for State Registration's legal-entity
registration certificate; tax/social-insurance registration is tracked
separately (specific tax-authority citation not independently confirmed
this session -- see `src/statute/facts.cljc`). Human-gated filing only.
